```npm
npx shadcn@latest add @kcn-seed/fieldset
```

## Usage

`Fieldset` is the frame that `CheckboxGroup` and `RadioGroup` are built from, styled like `Field`. Use it to group other controls, such as switches. Put `FieldsetHeader` (with `FieldsetLabel`, `FieldsetRequiredIndicator`, `FieldsetIndicatorText`), your controls, and `FieldsetFooter` (with `FieldsetDescription` or `FieldsetErrorMessage`) inside it.

`Fieldset` is a plain `div` and does not wire up accessibility for you: give it `role="group"` and point `aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby` at the label and messages.
