## Installation

```npm
npx shadcn@latest add @kcn-seed/bottom-sheet
```

## Usage

`BottomSheetContent` draws the sheet with an optional `title`, `description`, and close button; put content in `BottomSheetBody` and buttons in `BottomSheetFooter`. Swiping the sheet down more than a quarter of its height (or quickly) closes it, and so does pressing outside unless `closeOnInteractOutside` is `false`. There's no built-in action button — control `open` to close the sheet from your own buttons.
