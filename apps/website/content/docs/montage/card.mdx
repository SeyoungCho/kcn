## Installation

```npm
npx shadcn@latest add @kcn-montage/card
```

## Usage

The parts follow shadcn's Card, plus `CardMedia` for the thumbnail. The card has no surface of its own, and `render={<a />}` turns the whole card into a link.

```tsx
<Card>
  <CardMedia>
    <img src="/thumbnail.jpg" alt="" />
  </CardMedia>
  <CardHeader>
    <CardTitle>Title</CardTitle>
    <CardDescription>Caption</CardDescription>
  </CardHeader>
  <CardFooter>{/* badges */}</CardFooter>
</Card>
```
